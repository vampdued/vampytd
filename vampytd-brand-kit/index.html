Brand guidelines · v1.0 · November 2026

One play button, one clear voice.

Everything you need to use the VampYTD identity consistently: logo files, clear space, colour, type, app icons, favicons and social images.

01 · Logo

The logo

The mark is a rounded play-button ring with a solid triangle at its centre. The wordmark is set in Inter Black: Vamp in Ink, YTD in Vamp Red. Use the supplied files; never retype or redraw it.

Horizontal · primary

Default choice for headers, documents and video watermarks.

Stacked

For square or narrow spaces such as posters, covers and sign-in screens.

Wordmark

Only where the icon already appears nearby, for example next to an avatar.

Icon

Avatars, favicons, app tiles and any place the name is already clear.

Colourways and backgrounds

02 · Space & size

Clear space and minimum size

Keep a margin of x around the logo, where x is half the cap height of the V in the wordmark. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) enters that zone.

Clear space

xxxx

The same margin applies to the stacked logo and the icon (measured from the ring).

Minimum sizes

In print: horizontal 30 mm, stacked 18 mm, icon 5 mm. Below these sizes the ring gap closes up and the triangle stops reading.

05 · App & web

App icons, favicons and social

The icon in app tiles is optically centred: it sits slightly right of the geometric centre because the point of a play triangle carries less visual weight than its flat side.

Favicon & PWA setup

The SVG favicon swaps its triangle to white in dark browser themes. Paste into <head>:

<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="48x48">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">
<link rel="manifest" href="/site.webmanifest">
<meta name="theme-color" content="#0E0E12">

Social images

Open Graph 1200×630, X header 1500×500, YouTube banner 2560×1440 (logo sits in the 1546×423 safe area) and 800 px avatars. All in social/.
